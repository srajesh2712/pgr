Malin Head, Co. Donegal: Recorded the highest wind gust in Ireland during the storm at 115 km/h (71 mph / 62 knots), as well as the lowest barometric pressure reading at 969.6 hPa.   Knock Airport, Co. Mayo: Measured an extraordinary single-day rainfall total of 57.4 mm, breaking its all-time record for daily November rainfall.   Mace Head, Co. Galway & Belmullet, Co. Mayo: Logged the highest sustained 10-minute mean wind speeds during the event, reaching 89 km/h (48 knots).   Offshore Buoy M6: Anchored in the deep Atlantic off the west coast, it recorded massive maximum wave heights of 14.8 metres (approx. 48.5 ft).
