Stage Dynamics Breakdown

Frontogenesis
Surface Processes:

Straight stationary front separates polar cold air (flowing west) from warm tropical air (flowing east). Wind shear exists across boundary.

Aloft & Jet Stream:

Upper jet stream flows nearly straight zonal aloft. Divergence aloft is minimal; baroclinic instability is building.

Conveyor Belts & Clouds:

Parallel linear cloud band aligned along stationary front. Warm moist air is pushed horizontally rather than rising dynamically.
